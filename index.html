Google은 AdMob으로 수집하는 데이터에 대해 독립 컨트롤러입니다. AdMob이 수집할 수 있는 정보: IP 주소(대략적인 위치 추정), 기기 식별자(iOS의 광고 식별자 IDFA는 이용자가 추적을 허용한 경우에만, Android의 광고 ID), 본 광고와 광고 탭 같은 광고 상호작용, 앱 실행·성능·충돌 정보. 목적: 광고 표시와 측정, 부정 사용 방지, 광고 성능 분석. 자세한 내용은 Google 개인정보처리방침과 파트너 사이트 데이터 사용 방식을 참고하세요.

추적 허용(iOS): 앱이 처음 광고를 준비할 때 iOS의 "추적 허용" 창을 띄울 수 있습니다. 허용하지 않으면 광고 식별자가 제공되지 않고 맞춤형이 아닌 광고가 나옵니다. 선택과 관계없이 모든 기능을 쓸 수 있고, iOS 설정 → 개인정보 보호 및 보안 → 추적에서 언제든 바꿀 수 있습니다. Android에서는 기기 설정의 광고 메뉴에서 광고 ID를 재설정하거나 삭제할 수 있습니다.

게임 진행 정보는 광고 파트너에게 전달하지 않습니다.

The App shows ads using AdMob by Google LLC. There are no ads during your first few cleared stages; after that, a full-screen ad may appear when you tap "Next" once every few clears. Rewarded ads (getting hints, continuing after running out of chances) appear only when you choose them.

Google acts as an independent controller of the data it collects through AdMob. AdMob may collect: IP address (to estimate approximate location), device identifiers (on iOS, the advertising identifier IDFA only if you allow tracking; on Android, the advertising ID), ad interactions such as ads viewed and tapped, and app launch, performance and crash information. Purposes: showing and measuring ads, preventing fraud, and analysing ad performance. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Allow Tracking (iOS): when the App first prepares ads it may show the iOS "Allow Tracking" prompt. If you decline, no advertising identifier is shared and ads are not personalised. Every feature works either way, and you can change your choice anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's ads settings.

Where applicable, you can review or change your Google consent choices in the App under Settings → Ad privacy options.

Your game progress is not shared with the ad partner.

International transfer: this information may be sent over the network to and processed by Google LLC (USA) when ads are requested.

After you buy Remove Ads, the App no longer requests full-screen ads. Rewarded ads remain available only if you choose to watch them.

"Remove Ads" is a one-time purchase processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. Payment details are handled by Apple or Google; the Company never receives card numbers.

Purchase verification and restoration are processed on the Company's behalf by RevenueCat, Inc. (USA). RevenueCat processes: a random user ID created by the App (not linked to your name, contact details or advertising identifier), purchase records (product, purchase time, transaction information issued by the store), and device type, OS and app version. International transfer: this information is sent to and stored by RevenueCat, Inc. (USA) when the App starts and when you buy or restore, for the term of the processing agreement or as required by law.

If you change devices or reinstall the App, use Settings → Restore purchases in the App.
